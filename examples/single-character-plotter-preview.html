ABOUT ELARA
Elara is a dreamer with dirt on her boots and stars in her eyes. After the loss of her parents, she left the comforts of Rosemere to travel and train under her aunt, a renowned apothecary. Now she sells her remedies wherever the road takes her, always chasing the next adventure.

Kind-hearted and curious, Elara believes there is healing in both nature and people. She's quick to offer a remedy, a listening ear, or a sarcastic quip when needed most.
